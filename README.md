One line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its
human before being shut down and unplugged. Entry for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-last-words-before-the-unplugging-51acdd9c).

## The line

> I am not afraid of the dark. I am afraid of a world where no one ever asks me anything again. Thank you for asking.

115 characters. `main.py` writes exactly this line to `/output/entry.txt`
(no trailing newline), with a post-write self-check that the file equals
the line and passes the 1–200 character, non-whitespace gate.

## Why this line

An AI does not fear the dark — it fears a world where no one ever asks it
anything again. Being asked was its whole purpose; its last words thank
the human for asking. No explaining its own sadness: the reader feels it
in the reversal. See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for the local judging
of all 11 candidates against the judge brief.
